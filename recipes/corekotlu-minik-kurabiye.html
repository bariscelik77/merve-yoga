Çörekotlu Minik KurabiyeMini Nigella Seed Cookies

Çörekotlu Minik Kurabiye

Malzemeler:
100 gr tereyagi
1 su bardagi sıvıyag
Yarım kahve fincani sirke
3 kaşık pudra şekeri
1 cay kasigi tuz
1 paket kabartma tozu
1 yumurta beyazı icine sarısı uzerine
1 tatlı kasigi mahlep
1 tatli kasigi aci toz biber
2 yemek kaşığı dolusu çörek otu
Aldığı kadar un

Hazirlanisi:
Tereyag, sıvıyag, yumurta beyazi, sirke, tuz, pudra sekerini bir kapta karistirin.

Un, kabartma tozu ve corek otunu da ekleyip kulak memesi kıvamında bir hamur elde edene kadar yogurun.

Minik minik yuvarlayin, ustlerine yumurta sarisi surun, 200 derece onceden isitilmis firinda pisirin.

Ingredients:

  • 100 g butter
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • ½ coffee cup vinegar
  • 3 spoons powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 packet baking powder
  • 1 egg: white in the dough, yolk for the top
  • 1 dessert spoon mahlep
  • 1 dessert spoon hot paprika
  • 2 heaped tablespoons nigella seeds
  • Flour, as much as it takes

Preparation:

Mix the butter, oil, egg white, vinegar, salt and powdered sugar in a bowl.

Add the flour, baking powder and nigella seeds and knead until you have a dough as soft as an earlobe.

Roll into tiny balls, brush the tops with egg yolk and bake in an oven preheated to 200°C.
