Limonlu Vişneli KekLemon Sour Cherry Cake

Limonlu Vişneli Kek

Seker kullanmadan sadece pekmezle yaptim bu keki. Bence oldu. Bir de yine daha saglikli olsun diye yag olarak hindistan cevizi yagi kullandim. Artik her yerde cok kolay bulunuyor. Bence bu sekilde deneyin ama degisikliklere pek acik degilim diyorsaniz seker ve normal siviyag ile de cok guzel olacaktir. Zaten limon ve visne diyorum :) Gerisi bos. Simdiden ellerinize saglik.



Malzemeler:
3 adet buyuk yumurta
Yarim su bardagi pekmez
1 su bardagindan 2 parmak eksik sut
Yarim su bardagi hindistan cevizi yagi (erimis) veya siviyag
2 adet limonun kabugu
1 su bardagi dondurulmus visne
Yaklasik 3 su bardagi un
1 paket vanilin
1 paket kabartma tozu
1 parca rendelenmis taze zencefil

Hazirlanisi:
Firini 180 derecede isitin.
Visneleri tel suzgece alin. Suyu iyice suzulsun.
Yumurtalarla pekmezi 10 dk. yuksek devirde cirpin.
Sutu ve yagi ekleyip orta devirde mikserde cirpin.
Limon kabuklari ve rendelenmis zencefili ilave edin.
Kuru malzemeleri ayri bir kaba eleyin.
Visneleri cok az una bulayin ki pisirirken harcin icinde dibe cokmesin.
Unlu karisimi yavas yavas yumurtali karisima ekleyin.
Mikserde dusuk devirde 1 dk. kadar karistirin.
Spatula ile kenarda kalan hamuru da kasikla karistirin.
En son visneleri ekleyin ve bir kasikla tum kek hamurunu son kez karistirin.
Yaglanmis ve hafifce unlanmis kek kalibina dokun.
40-45 dakika pisirin.
Afiyet olsun.

I made this cake with only grape molasses (pekmez), no sugar, and I think it worked. To make it healthier still, I used coconut oil, which is easy to find everywhere now. I'd say try it this way, but if you're not keen on changes, it will be lovely with sugar and ordinary vegetable oil too. After all, it's lemon and sour cherry :) What more do you need?

Ingredients:

  • 3 large eggs
  • ½ cup grape molasses (pekmez)
  • 1 cup milk, minus two fingers
  • ½ cup coconut oil (melted) or vegetable oil
  • Zest of 2 lemons
  • 1 cup frozen sour cherries
  • About 3 cups flour
  • 1 packet vanilla
  • 1 packet baking powder
  • 1 piece of fresh ginger, grated

Preparation:

  • Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  • Put the sour cherries in a sieve and let them drain well.
  • Beat the eggs and molasses on high speed for 10 minutes.
  • Add the milk and oil and beat on medium speed.
  • Add the lemon zest and grated ginger.
  • Sift the dry ingredients into a separate bowl.
  • Toss the cherries in a little flour so they don't sink to the bottom while baking.
  • Slowly add the flour mixture to the egg mixture.
  • Mix on low speed for about 1 minute.
  • Scrape in the batter from the sides with a spatula.
  • Finally add the cherries and give the whole batter one last stir with a spoon.
  • Pour into a greased, lightly floured cake tin.
  • Bake for 40–45 minutes.
  • Enjoy!
