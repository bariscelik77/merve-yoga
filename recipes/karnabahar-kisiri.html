Karnabahar Kısırı

Malzemeler:

1 kucuk boy karnabahar

1 buyuk sogan

3 dis sarimsak

1 yemek kasigi domates salcasi

1 yemek kasigi biber salcasi

Zeytinyagi, tuz, kimyon, karabiber

Yarim limonun suyu

Dereotu, maydonoz, nane, kuru sogan

Hazirlanisi:

Kucuk parcalara ayirdiginiz karnabahari sirkeli suda biraz bekletin. Sonra iyice yikayip, rondodan bulgur kivamina gelene kadar cekin.

Sogan ve sarimsaklari da ayni sekilde rondodan gecirin.

Yanmaz bir tencerede karnabahar, sogan, sarimsak ve salcalari karnabaharlar biraz yumusayincaya kadar orta/kisik ateste pisirin. Cok az sicak su ekleyip de pisirebilirsiniz veya cok az zeytinyagi ile kavurarak da pisirebilirsiniz.

Karnabaharlar yumusayinca, ocagin altini kapatin sogumaya birakin.

Bu sirada kullanacaginiz yesillikleri ince ince dograyin.

Soguyan karnabahara yarim limonun suyunu, dilediginiz kadar baharat, zeytinyagi ve nar eksisini ilave edip iyice karistirin.

Yaninda bol marul ile servis edebilirsiniz.

Einize saglik.

Ingredients:

  • 1 small cauliflower
  • 1 large onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon pepper paste
  • Olive oil, salt, cumin, black pepper
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • Dill, parsley, mint, spring onion

Preparation:

Break the cauliflower into small florets and soak them briefly in water with a little vinegar. Then wash them well and pulse in a food processor until they look like bulgur.

Pulse the onion and garlic in the food processor the same way.

In a non-stick pot, cook the cauliflower, onion, garlic and pastes over medium-low heat until the cauliflower softens a little. You can add a splash of hot water, or sauté with just a little olive oil.

When the cauliflower is soft, turn off the heat and let it cool.

Meanwhile, finely chop the herbs you are using.

Add the lemon juice, spices to taste, olive oil and pomegranate molasses to the cooled cauliflower and mix well.

Serve with plenty of lettuce.

Enjoy!
