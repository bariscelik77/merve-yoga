Masala Baharatlı BezelyeGreen Peas Masala

Green Peas MasalaMasala Baharatlı Bezelye

Masala Hindistan'in buyulu baharat karisimi. Cayi ayri guzel, yemeklere katildiginda tum tadi tamamen degistiriyor. Ben bayiliyorum. Eger siz de denemek isterseniz, bezelyenizi bir de bu sekilde pisirin. Bakalim sizin de hosunuza gidecek mi?

Masalayi artik hemen hemen butun buyuk marketlerde bulabilirsiniz. Bulamazsaniz sayet, internette evde nasil hazirlanacagina dair bir suru tarif var. Size en uygun olanini secip denemenizi oneririm. Taze ve evde yapilaninin yerini hicbir ambalajli urun tutmaz unutmayin.

Masala is a magical Indian spice mixture. When you cook with it, the taste of the food changes completely. I adore this spice. If u want to try, i suggest you to cook your green peas in this way. Let's see what do you think? 

You can usually find masala in the spice section of grocery stores. Or, you might search the recipe from internet for the homemade one. Of course there is no comparison between packaged products and homemade!

Malzemeler/Ingredients:

Ayiklanmis 1 kg bezelye/1 kg shelled green peas

1 adet kup kup dogranmis havuc/1 carrot, diced

1 adet kup kup dogranmis patates/1 potato, diced

2 dis dogranmis sarimsak/2 cloves garlic, minced and diced

1 adet yemeklik dogranmis sogan/1 diced onion

Zeytinyagi/Olive oil

1 tatli kasigi domates salcasi/ 1/2 tablespoon tomato paste

1 tatli kasigi masala/1/2 tablespoon masala 

Tuz/Salt 

Hazirlanisi/Preperation:

Tencerede zaytinyagini isitinn. Sogan ve sarimsagi ekleyin. 3-4 dakika yuksek ateste renkleri donene kadar kavurun. Havuc ve patatesleri ilave edip 5 dakika daha karistirarak pisirin. Salca ve baharatlari bir kaseye koyun, uzerine ilik su ekleyip karistirin. Tencereye ilave edin. En son bezelyeleri de ekleyin, biraz daha su ilavesi yapin ve yaklasik 30 dakika orta ateste pisirin.

Heat the olive oil in a pot. Add onion and garlic and saute for 3-4 minutes on medium high heat. Add carrots and potatoes and cook for another 5 minutes. In another bowl, mix the spices and tomato paste. Pour warm water and mix well. Add the mixture to the pot and finally add the peas and more water if necessary, cook for 30 minutes at medium heat.

Masala is a magical Indian spice mix. It's lovely in tea, and added to food it changes the whole taste. I adore it. If you'd like to try it, cook your peas this way and see if you like it too.

You can find masala in most big supermarkets these days. If you can't, there are lots of recipes online for making it at home. Pick the one that suits you best. Remember, nothing packaged beats fresh and homemade.

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg shelled green peas
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 potato, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • Olive oil
  • 1 dessert spoon tomato paste
  • 1 dessert spoon masala
  • Salt

Preparation:

Heat the olive oil in a pot. Add the onion and garlic and sauté over high heat for 3–4 minutes until they colour. Add the carrot and potato and cook for another 5 minutes, stirring. Put the tomato paste and spices in a bowl, add warm water and mix. Add to the pot. Finally add the peas and a little more water, and cook over medium heat for about 30 minutes.
