Fırında Sebzeli Parmak TavukOven-Baked Chicken Fingers with Vegetables

Fırında Sebzeli Parmak Tavuk




Malzemeler:
2 adet tavuk gogus
1 yumurta
3 dis sarimsak
Balkabagi, pancar, patates
Galeta + misir unu karisimi
Tuz karabiber aci toz biber
Zerdecal
Sarimsak tozu
Zeytinyagi
Biberiye
1 adet limonun suyu

Hazirlanisi:
Tavuklari yikayip, kurulayin. Doverek inceltin ve parmak seklinde kesin. Limon suyuyla ovarak biraz dinlenmeye birakin.
Firini 180 derecede isitin.
Ayri bir kapta galeta ve misir ununi karistirin. Tuz, karabiber, toz kirmizi biber ve sarimsak tozunu ilave edin.
Sebzeleri iri iri kupler halinde dograyin. Cok az zeytinyagi, tuz, karabiber, zerdecal ve taze biberiye ekleyerek iyice karistirin. Firin tepsisine alin. Uzerlerine iri kesilmis sarimsaklari da koyun.
Tavuklari tek tek hafifce cirptiginiz yumurtaya sonra da unlu karisima bulayarak ayri bir firin tepsisine dizin.
180 derece firinda iki tepsiyi de 45 dakika pisirin.

Afiyet olsun.

Ingredients:

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 egg
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • Pumpkin, beetroot, potato
  • Breadcrumbs + corn flour mix
  • Salt, black pepper, hot paprika
  • Turmeric
  • Garlic powder
  • Olive oil
  • Rosemary
  • Juice of 1 lemon

Preparation:

Wash and dry the chicken. Pound it thin and cut into fingers. Rub with the lemon juice and let it rest for a while.

Preheat the oven to 180°C.

In a separate bowl, mix the breadcrumbs and corn flour. Add salt, black pepper, paprika and garlic powder.

Cut the vegetables into large cubes. Mix well with a little olive oil, salt, pepper, turmeric and fresh rosemary. Put them on a baking tray and scatter the roughly chopped garlic on top.

Dip the chicken pieces one by one into the lightly beaten egg, then into the flour mixture, and arrange them on a separate baking tray.

Bake both trays at 180°C for 45 minutes.

Enjoy!
