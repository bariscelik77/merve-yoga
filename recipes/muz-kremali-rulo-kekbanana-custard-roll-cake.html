Muz Kremalı Rulo KekBanana Custard Roll Cake

Banana Custard Roll CakeMuz Kremalı Rulo Kek

Tarifi bu siteden aldim. Kendimden hicbirsey katmadim. Aynen ne diyorsa o sekilde uyguladim ve sonuc cok guzel oldu. Hem krema, hem kek tarifi ikisi de super. Hazirlanisi icin sitedeki videolari izleyebilirsiniz. Bir sonraki denememde baska meyvelerle de deneyecegim.

I got the recipe from this site.  I didn't add anything. I exactly did what it's said and the result is delicious. Both the custard and the cake is great. You can watch the videos for preparation. Next time, I will try other fruits for the custard as well.

Malzemeler/Ingredients:

Kremasi Icin/For Custard Cream:

1 adet buyuk muz/ 1 large banana
1 yumurta/ 1 egg
1 yemek kasigi misir nisastasi/ 1 tablespoon of corn starch
3 yemek kasigi toz seker/ 3 tablespoon of granulated sugar
150ml sut/ 150 ml milk

Sunger Kek icin/For Sponge Cake:

2 yumurta/ 2 eggs
50g pudra sekeri/50 gr powdered sugar
35g un/ 35g flour
20ml sut/ 20ml milk

I got the recipe from this site. I didn't add anything of my own; I did exactly what it said and the result was delicious. Both the custard and the cake are great. You can watch the videos on the site for the method. Next time I'll try the custard with other fruits as well.

Ingredients:

For the custard cream:

  • 1 large banana
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon corn starch
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 150 ml milk

For the sponge cake:

  • 2 eggs
  • 50 g powdered sugar
  • 35 g flour
  • 20 ml milk
