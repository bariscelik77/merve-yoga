Mini Cheesecake


Malzemeler:

200 gr labne peyniri

4-5 adet eti burcak biskuvi

20 gr erimis tereyagi

100 gr Pinar Beyaz

100 ml krema

75 gr toz seker

1 adet yumurta

1 cay kasigi sivi vanilya

1 tatli kasigi limon suyu

1 adet limonun rendelenmis kabugu

1 yemek kasigi un

Hazirlanisi

Tum malzemeleri oda sicakligina gelinceye kadar bekletin.
Muffin kaliplarina kagitlari yerlestirin.
Firini 180 derecede isitin.
Biskuvileri erimis tereyagi ile rondodan gecirin ve kagitlarin tabanina paylastirin.
Peynirleri, kremayi, sekeri, vanilya ve limon kabuklarini dusuk devirde tummalzemeler karisana kadar cirpin. Yumurtayi ilave edin. En son limon suyu ve unu da ekleyip, son kez karistirin.
Karisimi kagitlara dokun. Piserken kabarmayacagi icin cok fazla bosluk birakmaniza gerek yok.
Muffin kalibini firin tepsisine yerlestirin, tepsiye 1 parmak su koyun. 20-25 dakika kadar pisirin. Firinin kapagini acin ve cheesecakeleri bu sekilde sogutun. Soguyunca 1 gece buzdolabinda bekletin. Servis ederken uzerine istediginiz meyveyi ekleyip servis yapabilirsiniz. Afiyet olsun.

Ingredients:

  • 200 g labneh cheese
  • 4–5 Eti Burçak (digestive-style) biscuits
  • 20 g melted butter
  • 100 g Pınar Beyaz (spreadable white cheese)
  • 100 ml cream
  • 75 g granulated sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon liquid vanilla
  • 1 dessert spoon lemon juice
  • Grated zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 tablespoon flour

Preparation:

Let all the ingredients come to room temperature.

Put paper cases in a muffin tin.

Preheat the oven to 180°C.

Blitz the biscuits with the melted butter and divide between the bottoms of the cases.

Beat the cheeses, cream, sugar, vanilla and lemon zest on low speed until combined. Add the egg. Finally add the lemon juice and flour and mix one last time.

Pour the mixture into the cases. They don't rise while baking, so you don't need to leave much space.

Put the muffin tin on an oven tray and pour a finger's depth of water into the tray. Bake for 20–25 minutes. Open the oven door and let the cheesecakes cool like that. Once cool, leave them in the fridge overnight. Serve topped with any fruit you like. Enjoy!
