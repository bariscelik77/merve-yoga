Enginarlı Ispanaklı CheesecakeArtichoke Spinach Cheesecake

Artichoke Spinach CheesecakeEnginarlı Ispanaklı Cheesecake


Hic tuzlu cheesecake denediniz mi? Denemelisiniz!

Eger bir seferde bitiremezseniz, uzerine cok az zeytinyagi gezdirip firinda 15 dakika kadar isitabilir ve tekrar tekrar bu keyfi yasayabilirsiniz. Yaraticilik size kalmis belki kuru domates veya biraz dogranmis zeytin de eklenebilir. Guzel bir sarap esliginde haslanmis brokoli, kereviz saplari ve ince uzun dogranmis havuc ile servis yapabilirsiniz. Simdiden afiyet olsun.


Have you ever try savoury cheesecake before? You should!!

If you have leftover cheesecake, drizzle a little extra virgin olive oil on top and reheat in the oven for 15 minutes and serve hot You can serve your cheesecake with a good wine accompanied with boiled broccoli, celery sticks, and carrot sticks. Bon apetit!




MALZEMELER/ INGREDIENTS


  • 20 cm kelepceli kek kalibi / 8 inch cake pan

  • 1 su bardagi dogranmis ispanak / 1 cup shredded spinach

  • 1 su bardagi krem peynir / 1 cup cream cheese

  • 1 su bardagi ricotta peyniri veya lor peynir / 1 cup ricotta cheese

  • 120 gr ezine peyniri / 120 gr feta cheese

  • Yarim su bardagi iri iri dogranmis enginar kalbi / 1/2 cup of artichoke hearts (chopped)

  • 1 buyuk dis sarisak (rendelenmis) / 1 large clove of garlic (minced)

  • 2 adet yumurta / 2 eggs

  • 1 adet yesil sogan / 1 chopped green onion


Tum malzemeler oda sicakliginda oldugunda en iyi sonucu alirsiniz. Use all the ingredients at room temperature for the best result.

Alt taban icin / For the cheesecake crust:


  • 100 gr (yaklasik 1,5 paket) cizi kraker / 100 gr salty crackers

  • 50 gr eritilmis tereyagi / 50 gr melted butter

  • DIlerseniz dogranmis taze kekik veya biberiye / Fresh chopped rosemarin or oregon can be used




HAZIRLANISI/ INSTRUCTIONS:


  1. Firini 175 derecede isitin. / Preheat your oven to 175 degrees.

  2. Ispanak ve enginarlari yikayin ve suyunu elinizle sikarak iyice suzun. / Wash spinach and artichokes and be sure to fully drain both. Squeeze with your hand if necessary. This prevents moisture.

  3. Kalibinizi hafif yaglayarak hazirlayin. / Prepare your cake with lightly greasing with butter.

  4. Cheesecake'in alt tabani icin krakerleri ezin ve erimis tereyagini ekleyin. Kek kalibinin tabanina bastirarak yerlestirin. Firinda 10 dakika pirisin. For the crust, crush crackers and combine with melted butter. Spread evenly at the bottom of the cake pan and bake for 10 minutes.

  5. Krem peynir, ricotta ve catalla ezdiginiz ezine peynirini mutfak robotunda iyice karistirin. / Combine cream cheese, ricotta, and feta in your food processor.

  6. Yumurtalari teker teker ilave edin. Arkasindan ispanak, enginar kalbi ve sarimsagi da ekleyerek dusuk devirde cok az karistirin. / Next add the eggs one by one, spinach, artichoke hearts and garlic and gently mix.

  7. Son olarak dogradiginiz yesil sogani da karisima ekleyin. / Lastly, add the chopped green onion into the mixture.

  8. Hazirladiginiz peynirli karisimi kek kalibiniza, krakerli tabanin uzerine dokun. / Pour the cheese mixture onto the crust.

  9. Yaklasik 50 dk pisirin. / Bake approximately for 50 minutes.

  10. 15 dakika bekleyin ve yavasca kalibtan cikartin. / Leave the cheesecake for 15 minutes to cool down and very gently remove from the pan.


Have you ever tried a savoury cheesecake? You should!

If you can't finish it in one go, drizzle a little olive oil over it and warm it in the oven for about 15 minutes, and enjoy it all over again. Get creative: you could add sun-dried tomatoes or some chopped olives. Serve with a good wine, alongside steamed broccoli, celery sticks and thin carrot sticks. Enjoy!

Ingredients:

  • 20 cm springform cake tin
  • 1 cup chopped spinach
  • 1 cup cream cheese
  • 1 cup ricotta or lor (curd cheese)
  • 120 g Ezine (feta-style) cheese
  • ½ cup roughly chopped artichoke hearts
  • 1 large clove garlic, grated
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 spring onion

You'll get the best result if all the ingredients are at room temperature.

For the base:

  • 100 g (about 1½ packs) Çizi crackers
  • 50 g melted butter
  • Chopped fresh thyme or rosemary, if you like

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C.
  2. Wash the spinach and artichokes and squeeze out the water well by hand.
  3. Lightly grease your tin.
  4. For the base, crush the crackers and add the melted butter. Press into the bottom of the tin and bake for 10 minutes.
  5. Blend the cream cheese, ricotta and fork-mashed Ezine cheese well in a food processor.
  6. Add the eggs one at a time. Then add the spinach, artichoke hearts and garlic and mix briefly on low speed.
  7. Finally, stir in the chopped spring onion.
  8. Pour the cheese mixture over the cracker base in the tin.
  9. Bake for about 50 minutes.
  10. Leave for 15 minutes, then gently remove from the tin.
