Fırın MücverBaked Zucchini Fritters (Mücver)

Fırın Mücver


Malzemeler:

2 adet rendelenmis kabak (tel suzgecte biraz bekletin veya elinizle suyunu iyice sıkın)

3-4 dal yeşil soğan
1 yumurta
Maydanoz, dereotu
2 yemek kasigi zeytinyag

1 çay fincanı un

Karabiber
1 çay bardağı beyaz peynir
1 çay k. kabartma tozu

Hazirlanisi: 
Rendelediginiz kabaklari bir kaseye alin. Uzerine ince ince dogranmis yesil sogan, yumurta,kiyilmis maydanoz,un, dereotu, karabiber, ufalanmis beyaz peyniri koyup elinizle karistirin. 2 yemek kasigi zeytinyagi ekleyin yagli kagit serili firin tepsisisne yuvarlayip dizin.

Onceden isitilmis 180 derece firinda 35-40 dakika kizarana kadar pisirin.

Ingredients:

  • 2 grated zucchini (leave them in a sieve for a while, or squeeze out the water well by hand)
  • 3–4 spring onions
  • 1 egg
  • Parsley, dill
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teacup flour
  • Black pepper
  • 1 tea glass of white cheese (feta)
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Preparation:

Put the grated zucchini in a bowl. Add the finely chopped spring onion, egg, chopped parsley, flour, dill, black pepper and crumbled white cheese and mix by hand. Add 2 tablespoons olive oil, then shape into rounds and arrange them on a baking tray lined with baking paper.

Bake in a preheated 180°C oven for 35–40 minutes, until golden.
