Bademli Limonlu Kurabiye

Bademli limonlu kurabiye:


  • Iri cekilmis cig badem

  • 2 limonun kabugu (rendelenmis)

  • 1 yemek kasigi suzme yogurt

  • 1 paket kabartma tozu

  • Un (2 su bardagindan biraz fazla)

  • 1 su bardagindan biraz az toz seker

  • 1 tatli kasigi tarcin

  • 2 yumurta

  • 175 gr tereyagi (oda sicakliginda)


Hazirlanisi:

Firini 175 derecede isitin. 

Firin tepsisine yagli kagit serin. 

Un, tarcin, kabartma tozunu genis bir kaba eleyin. Badem ve limon kabuklarini ilave edip bir kasikla karistirin. 

Unlu karisimin ortasini acin, yumurta, seker, yogurt ve yagi ortadaki havuza koyup elinizle iyice karistirin. Azar azar unlu karisimdan alarak hamuru yogurun. Un az gelirse eleyerek ilave edin. 

Ele yapismayan yumusak bir hamur olana kadar elinizle yogurun. 

Minik toplar taparak firin tepsisine dizin. 

Catalla hafifce ortalarina bastirip, 25 dakika pisirin.

Almond lemon cookies:

  • Coarsely chopped raw almonds
  • Zest of 2 lemons (grated)
  • 1 tablespoon strained (Greek) yogurt
  • 1 packet baking powder
  • Flour (a little more than 2 cups)
  • A little less than 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 dessert spoon cinnamon
  • 2 eggs
  • 175 g butter (room temperature)

Preparation:

Preheat the oven to 175°C.

Line a baking tray with baking paper.

Sift the flour, cinnamon and baking powder into a large bowl. Add the almonds and lemon zest and stir with a spoon.

Make a well in the middle of the flour mixture, put the eggs, sugar, yogurt and butter into the well and mix well by hand. Knead the dough, drawing in the flour mixture little by little. If it needs more flour, sift in a little more.

Knead by hand until you have a soft dough that doesn't stick to your hands.

Roll into small balls and arrange them on the baking tray.

Press the middles lightly with a fork and bake for 25 minutes.
