Terbiyeli Tavuk ÇorbasıChicken Soup with Egg-Lemon Sauce

Terbiyeli Tavuk Çorbası



Malzemeler:
1 adet koy tavugu (1 kg)
1 adet sogan
2-3 adet defne yapragi
1 kucuk kahve fincani tel sehriye
1 yumurtanin sarisi
1 adet limonun suyu
Maydonoz

Hazirlanisi:
Tavugu tencerede sogan ve defne yapragi ile haslayin. Haslaninca defne yapragi ve sogani cikartin. Tavugu kucuk parcalara ayirin. Tavuk suyuna eristeyi atin, yumusayana kadar pisirin. Tavuklari da ekleyin. Tuzunu, karabiberini koyun. Ayri bir kapta limon ve yumurta sarisini iyice cirpin. Tavuk suyundan bir kepce ilave edin ve corbayi karistirirken terbiyeyi yavas yavas corbaya dokun. Bu arada karistirmayi birakmayin ki kesilmesin. Terbiyesini de ekledikten sonra kisik ateste 5 dakika daha pisirin. Maydonoz, bol karabiber ve limon suyu ile servis edin.

Ingredients:

  • 1 free-range chicken (1 kg)
  • 1 onion
  • 2–3 bay leaves
  • 1 small coffee cup of vermicelli
  • 1 egg yolk
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Parsley

Preparation:

Boil the chicken in a pot with the onion and bay leaves. When it's cooked, remove the bay leaves and onion. Shred the chicken into small pieces. Add the vermicelli to the broth and cook until soft. Add the chicken back in, and season with salt and pepper. In a separate bowl, whisk the lemon juice and egg yolk well. Add a ladle of broth, then slowly pour this sauce into the soup while stirring. Keep stirring the whole time so it doesn't curdle. Once the sauce is in, cook over low heat for 5 more minutes. Serve with parsley, plenty of black pepper and lemon juice.
