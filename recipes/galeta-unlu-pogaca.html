Galeta Unlu PoğaçaPoğaça with Breadcrumb Coating

Galeta Unlu Poğaça

Malzemeler: 

1 su bardagi siviyag

2 su bardagi yogurt

3 paket kabartma tozu 

Un (yaklasik 4,5-5 su bardagi)

Tuz 

Ici icin: tatli ayvalik loru 

Disi icin: Galeta unu

Hazirlanisi:

Un, kabartma tozu ve tuzu karistirin. Bir kaba eleyin. Ortasini acin. Sivi malzemeleri koyun, iyice yogurun. Yumusak ele yapismayan bir hamur elde edene kadar yavas yavas un ekleyin. Hamuru biraz dinlendirdikten sonra, hamurdan ceviz buyuklugu parcalar kopartip ortasina lor peynirini koyun ve yuvarlayin. Galeta ununa bulayin, firin tepsisine dizin. Onceden isitilmis 180 derece firinda yaklasik 20 dk. pisirin. 

   

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 cups yogurt
  • 3 packets baking powder
  • Flour (about 4½–5 cups)
  • Salt
  • For the filling: sweet Ayvalık lor (curd cheese)
  • For the outside: breadcrumbs

Preparation:

Mix the flour, baking powder and salt and sift into a bowl. Make a well in the middle, add the liquid ingredients and knead well. Add flour little by little until you have a soft dough that doesn't stick to your hands. Let the dough rest a little, then pinch off walnut-sized pieces, put some curd cheese in the middle and roll into balls. Coat them in breadcrumbs and arrange on a baking tray. Bake in a preheated 180°C oven for about 20 minutes.
