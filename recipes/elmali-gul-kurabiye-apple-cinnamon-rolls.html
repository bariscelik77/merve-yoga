Apple Cinnamon RollsElmalı Gül Kurabiye

 

Hamur için malzemeler:

  • 250 gr oda sıcaklığında tereyag
  • 1 cay bbardagi yogurt
  • 4 yemek kkasigi pudra sekeri
  • Un (yaklasik 3,5 su bardagi)
  • 1 cay kasigi  karbonat
  • 1 paket vanilya

İç Harci:

  • 4 adet orta boy elma (3 yesil+1 sari veya kirmizi  karistirinca daha guzel oluyor)
  • 1 yemek kasigi tarcin
  • 4 yemek kasigi toz seker
  • 1 su bardagi iri cekilmis ceviz

*Not: Benim elmalarım buyuk oldugu icin ic malzeme biraz arttı. Sizin kullanacaginiz elmalar da büyükse 3 adet yeterli olacaktır, veya hamuru 2 olcu olarak hazirlayabilirsiniz.

Hazirlanisi:
Once kurabiyelerin ic harcini hazirlayin. Elmaların kabuklarini soyup, rendeleyin. Bu sirada rendelenmis elmalarin uzerine biraz limon suyu sıkarsanız kararmasını onleyebilirsiniz.
Bir tencereye elma rendesi, toz seker, tarcin ve ceviz icini koyun ve elmalar suyunu birakip cekene kadar pisirin. Pisen harci ocaktan alın ve sogumaya birakin.

Bu sirada hamurunuzu hazirlayin. Genis bir karıştırma kabına tereyagi, yogurt ve pudra sekerini koyun, elinizle iyice karistirin. Daha sonra un, karbonat ve vanilyayı ilave edin. Ele yapismayan yumusak bir hamur olacak.

Hamuru streç filme sarıp, 20-30 dk. buzdolabında dinlendirin.
Bu sırada fırını 180 derecede ısıtın.
Dinlenen hamuru 2 parçaya bolun ve her parcayi 1 cm kinliginda, dikdortgen bicimde acin.
Hamuru bicakla enine esit parcalara kesin. Hamurun uzerine biraz toz seker ve tarcin serpip ic harci yayin. Her parcayi rulo seklinde sararak gul sekli verin. Yagli kagit serilmis firin tepsisine aralikli şeklinde yerlestirin ve surekli kontrol ederek pisirin. Yaklasik 25-30 dakika suruyor ama surekli kontrol etmenizi oneriririm.

Altlari pismis olsun ama kurabiyeler biraz yumusak olabilir. Soguyunca daha da cekiyorlar. Afiyet olsun.

Ingredients for the dough:

  • 250 g butter at room temperature
  • 1/4 cup yogurt
  • 4 tablespoon powdered sugar
  • Flour (about 3+1/2 cup)
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 packege of vanilla

Filling:

  • 4 medium apples (3 green + 1 yellow or red)
  • 1 tbsp cinnamon
  • 4 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 cup crushed walnuts
  • Note: My apples are bigger so the filling increased slightly. If your apples are big too 3 will be enough, or you can prepare the dough as double size.

Preparation:
Peel  and grate the apples.
Place grated apples, powdered sugar, cinnamon and walnuts into a cooking pan and cook 5-6 minutes. Let cool.

For the dough, put butter in a large mixing bowl, place the yogurt and powdered sugar and mix by hand.
Then, add bicarbonate soda and vanilla. Slowly add flour until you get soft and unsticky dough.

Wrap the dough and refrigerate 20-30 minutes.
Meanwhile preheat the oven at 180 degrees.
Divide the dough in 2 parts and roll each 1 cm thick in rectangular shape.
Cut the dough horizontally  into equal pieces with a knife. Sprinkle sugar and cinnamon over the dough and put the filling. Roll the dough (form of rose) and place onto oven tray and cook  approximately 25-30 minutes, but I suggest you to check frequently.

Bottoms should be baked but let little soft in the middle. They will become hard as they get cool. Bon Appetit.

Ingredients for the dough:

  • 250 g butter at room temperature
  • ¼ cup yogurt
  • 4 tablespoons powdered sugar
  • Flour (about 3½ cups)
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 packet vanilla

Filling:

  • 4 medium apples (3 green + 1 yellow or red is a nicer mix)
  • 1 tablespoon cinnamon
  • 4 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1 cup coarsely crushed walnuts

*Note: My apples were big, so I had a bit more filling. If your apples are big too, 3 will be enough, or you can make a double batch of dough.

Preparation:

Make the filling first. Peel and grate the apples. A squeeze of lemon juice over the grated apple stops it going brown.

Put the grated apple, sugar, cinnamon and walnuts in a pan and cook until the apples release their juice and it evaporates. Take off the heat and let cool.

Meanwhile make the dough. Put the butter, yogurt and powdered sugar in a large bowl and mix well by hand. Then add the flour, baking soda and vanilla. You'll get a soft dough that doesn't stick to your hands.

Wrap the dough in cling film and rest it in the fridge for 20–30 minutes. Meanwhile preheat the oven to 180°C.

Divide the rested dough in two and roll each piece into a rectangle about 1 cm thick.

Cut the dough crosswise into equal strips with a knife. Sprinkle a little sugar and cinnamon over the dough and spread the filling on top. Roll up each strip into a rose shape. Place them spaced apart on a tray lined with baking paper and bake, checking often. It takes about 25–30 minutes, but I recommend keeping an eye on them.

The bottoms should be baked, but the cookies can stay a little soft; they firm up as they cool. Enjoy!
